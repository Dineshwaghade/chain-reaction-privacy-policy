Privacy Policy

Chain Reaction · Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Chain Reaction mobile app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. Chain Reaction is an offline, single/multi-device strategy game. We built it to work without requiring an account, login, or personal profile of any kind.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information. There is no user profile, no chat, and no user-generated content that is uploaded or shared.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To remember your preferences and stats between sessions, the App saves the following directly on your device (never transmitted to us):

  • Sound and vibration on/off preferences
  • Preferred board size, AI difficulty, and light/dark theme
  • Local play statistics such as matches played, best combo, and total explosions

This data is stored using standard Android local storage and is removed automatically if you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising

The App may display ads served through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may automatically collect certain technical information (such as device identifiers, general location, and app usage data) to serve and measure ads, in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites and apps that use our services

You can limit ad personalization through your device's ad settings (e.g. Settings → Google → Ads on Android).

4. App Integrity Services

The App may use Firebase App Check to help verify that requests to backend services come from our genuine, unmodified app. This process does not collect personal information and is used solely for security purposes. Analytics collection is disabled in this app.

5. Permissions We Use

  • Internet / Network State — required to load ads
  • Vibrate — used for haptic feedback when placing orbs and during explosions; can be turned off in Settings

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information collected by third-party services described above (AdMob) is handled under those providers' own privacy policies, not by us directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
